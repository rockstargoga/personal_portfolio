I work in the gap between design and manufacturing - the place where prototypes have to prove they can survive the factory floor. My job is to build quality systems that help early concepts scale into reliable, manufacturable products. That means defining how we measure success before mass production begins - turning trial and error into repeatable, data-driven decisions. I’m constantly experimenting with new tools, analytics platforms, and AI-driven insights to make problem solving faster and more objective. The goal is simple: build systems that get smarter over time

I like the early stages when things are half working and half chaos. That’s when design, data, and people all collide, and you can still influence how things get built. I’m driven by curiosity and adaptability - understanding why things fail, and how to make them fail less next time. I value clarity, accountability, and quiet rigor over buzzwords. For me, good engineering is continuous learning. Every build cycle is an iteration not just for the product, but for the people and systems behind it.
